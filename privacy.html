TOPS privacy

Updated 9 November 2026

TOPS is a free browser darts game made by Hayden Kelly for mates. It is not a business.

What is stored

Your game save (career progress, settings, your player's look and name) is kept in your browser. If you sign in with Google, the same save is also stored in a database (Supabase, hosted in Tokyo) against your Google account ID so it can follow you between devices. Google sign-in gives the game your name, email address and profile picture URL; the game shows your first name and keeps the email only as part of the account record. Nothing else from your Google account is read.

Leaderboard

If you play Career matches, your chosen player name, tier, match count, wins, averages, 180s and best finish are published on the in-game leaderboard, which anyone with the game link can see. Use a nickname if you prefer.

Online matches

Live matches send your dart positions and player name to the other player through a realtime channel. Nothing from a match is kept after it ends apart from the result on your own save.

No tracking, no ads

There are no analytics, ads or tracking cookies. The game loads from GitHub Pages and talks only to Supabase and, if you sign in, Google.

Delete your data

Message Hayden or email hdnkelly@gmail.com and your account, save and leaderboard row are deleted.
