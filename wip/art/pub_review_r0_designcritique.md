## Design Critique: Pub venue (tier 0), live 1.83 baseline

### Overall Impression
Already characterful: exposed brick, a neon sign, framed posters and a patterned pub carpet say "local" straight away. The problem is exposure: everything is evenly lit and fairly bright, so it reads like a showroom pub, and next to the new Club it now looks brighter and more polished than the tier above.

### Usability (match readability)
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Busy red brick sits directly behind the thrower's head and arm; the white polo reads, the skin tones and dart less so | 🟡 Moderate | Darken and desaturate the brick behind the thrower; keep the neon as the bright accent |
| Patterned red carpet is loud on phone, competes with the HUD bottom edge | 🟡 Moderate | Mute and darken the carpet, add wear and stains |
| Neon sign sits under the HR panel on desktop | 🟢 Minor | Layout, not art; note for Polish |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **Eye goes first to:** the thrower and the neon (fine), but the brick texture pulls attention everywhere else.
- No light pooling: the board end isn't brighter than the walls, so the reference ("the board is the brightest thing in a dim room") isn't met.

### Consistency (against the ladder)
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Overall brightness | Pub is now brighter than Club | Pub exposure should sit at or below Club; warmer, dimmer, more contrast |
| Materials | Clean brick and carpet | Add grime: darker grout, soot near the ceiling, beer stains near the bar and oche |

### What Works Well
- Neon, posters and brick give identity at a glance.
- The thrower's white polo separates well.

### Priority Recommendations
1. **Light hierarchy:** dim room, warm pool on the board and oche, neon as the accent light.
2. **Brick:** darker, less saturated, sooty toward the ceiling.
3. **Carpet:** muted and stained, quieter pattern.
4. **Bar and pendants:** warm glow pockets so the dim room still feels inviting.
