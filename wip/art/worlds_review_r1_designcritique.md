## Design Critique: World Championship crowd (tier 6), pass 1 vs live

### Overall Impression
Live reads as a toy arena: identical brown balls on boxes, evenly lit, so the top tier looks emptier and cheaper than Regional and DPNZ (which have real people since 1.92). Pass 1 fills the same stands with atlas people, and it finally feels like a sold-out final.

### Usability (match readability)
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Live: ball heads sit right behind the thrower's head and dart hand, same tone as skin | 🟡 Moderate (fixed) | Darker, cooler crowd: the bald head and white shirt now separate |
| Pass 1: the atlas's orange hi-vis tops still pop behind the throwing arm | 🟢 Minor | Watch ChatGPT's call; could skip those atlas columns in the first two rows |
| Crowd is dense behind the dart at the top of the backswing | 🟢 Minor | Acceptable at Worlds (packed is the point); the dart's red flight still reads |

### Visual Hierarchy
- Eye goes to the thrower first, then the WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP wall, then the crowd. Correct.
- Depth: back rows fade into the haze; one tone step per row.

### Consistency (ladder)
- People now at every staged tier from Regional up; Worlds is the densest, as it should be.

### What Works Well
- One draw call; the same cheer logic as the main crowd.

### Verdict (pass 1)
Big step from live; wait on ChatGPT's scores before locking.
