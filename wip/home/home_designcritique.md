## Design Critique: TOPS career home, three concepts vs today (390×844, mid-career save, round 2)

### Today (baseline)
The art is strong but the page is a stack of equal blocks. "FILL-IN" is jargon shouted at 64 px. Nothing says where you stand in the league or why tonight matters. Level progress and contracts sit below the fold. The player figure crowds the headline, and the labels are 11–12 px caps.

### A · Tonight (one big next action)
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Tale of the tape answers "who and how am I doing" in one glance | 🟢 works | Keep |
| The stake ("Win and you go 2nd") is the reason to tap, now the headline tile | 🟢 fixed in r2 | Keep |
| The three "also" tiles stack three lines of 14 px text each | 🟡 | One line plus value per tile if it goes to a build |
- **Hierarchy:** name → tape → stake → Walk on → extras. Correct order. Walk on sits in the thumb zone at 72 px tall.
- **Accessibility:** no text under 11 px; lime on dark ≈ 12:1; all taps ≥ 44 px.

### B · Your local (the pub is the menu)
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Most memorable; the career finally feels like a place | 🟢 | Keep the room, not a menu |
| Places can read as a scavenger hunt | 🟡 fixed in r2 | 5 places; lime = go-to (career, practice, money), white = shop/kit |
| Hotspots sit on a still image | 🟡 | A build would use the live 3D pub, with the camera easing to a place on tap |
- **Hierarchy:** chalkboard fixture first (bottom, thumb zone), then lime places, then white.
- **Accessibility:** labels 16–19 px, tags ≥ 44 px tall; the board is no longer covered.

### C · Career dashboard (progress at a glance)
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Header tells the story ("3rd · 1 pt off 2nd") instead of raw stats | 🟢 fixed in r2 | Keep |
| Walk on banner is now second in reading order | 🟢 fixed in r2 | Keep |
| Densest of the three; trophy shelf is low value | 🟢 minor | Drop the shelf first if space is needed |
- **Hierarchy:** story → play → table → rival → cards/contract.
- **Accessibility:** table 14 px, tiles 12–26 px; all ≥ 11 px.

### Distinctness check
A = one decision and play. B = explore a place. C = manage a career. Different jobs, different layouts, the same art and type.
